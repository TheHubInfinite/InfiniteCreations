1. Who we are

Infinite Creations is an independent digital studio based in India, building websites, custom software and automation for small and mid-sized businesses. This policy applies to infinitecreations.in and to any enquiry you send us by email, phone, WhatsApp, or our contact form, regardless of where in the world you are contacting us from.

2. What data we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us. We do not require you to create an account to browse this site.

Information you provide directly

  • Your name, email address and phone number, when you fill in our contact form, email us, call us or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Details about your business and project, when you describe what you need in an enquiry.
  • Any other information you choose to include in a message to us.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical information such as your browser type, device type, approximate location (country/city level) and pages visited, if analytics tools are enabled on this site.
  • We do not use this information to personally identify you, and we do not sell it.
Our contact form: when you press send, the details you typed are sent securely to our inbox through Formspree, a third-party form-processing service. Formspree processes them on our behalf and its own privacy policy also applies. If the form cannot send for any reason, the site offers to open your own email app instead, and nothing is sent until you press send there.

3. Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare quotes, proposals and contracts.
  • To deliver work you've commissioned from us, and provide support afterwards.
  • To send you updates about a project you're actively discussing with us (not marketing, unless you separately opt in).
  • To understand, in aggregate, how people use our site, so we can improve it.
  • To meet our legal, accounting and tax obligations.

4. Our legal basis for using it

Wherever you're writing to us from, we only use your data where we have a valid reason to, including:

  • Consent — you gave us your details voluntarily by contacting us.
  • Contract — we need your details to quote, agree and deliver a project with you.
  • Legitimate interest — responding to enquiries and keeping our site secure and working well.
  • Legal obligation — for example, retaining invoices for tax purposes.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We may share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run our business — for example, Formspree (processes contact-form submissions), Netlify (hosts this website and may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages), email, accounting and payment providers — who may use your data only to provide that service to us.
  • Professional advisors such as accountants or legal counsel, where necessary.
  • Authorities, where we are legally required to disclose information (for example, in response to a valid court order).
  • A successor business, only if Infinite Creations is ever sold or restructured, and only with the same protections carried forward.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, or for the duration of an active project plus a reasonable period afterwards for support and legal/accounting records (typically up to 7 years for financial records, in line with Indian tax law). If you ask us to delete your data and we have no legal reason to keep it, we will.

7. Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use tracking cookies or advertising pixels, and its fonts are served from our own website rather than a third party. If we add analytics (such as Google Analytics or a privacy-friendly alternative) in future, we will update this policy to name the tool and explain the choices available to you, including how to opt out where applicable.

8. Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you generally have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to delete your data, where we're not legally required to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example by asking us to stop contacting you.
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your data.

To exercise any of these, just email us — see Contact below. We'll respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within the timeframes required by applicable law.

9. Additional notice for clients in India

For users in India, we process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and applicable Indian IT rules. Under the DPDPA, you ("Data Principal") have the right to access, correct, and request erasure of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity. We act as the "Data Fiduciary" for information you share with us and take reasonable security safeguards to protect it, as described in Section 11.

If you have an unresolved grievance, you may contact our grievance contact at thehubinfinite@gmail.com before escalating to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Additional notice for international clients

If you are contacting us from the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, we aim to handle your data consistently with the principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the legal bases described in Section 4 and the rights described in Section 8, along with the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

If you are contacting us from California or elsewhere in the United States, we do not sell personal data, and you may request details of what we hold about you or ask for it to be deleted, consistent with applicable state privacy laws (such as the CCPA/CPRA).

11. How we protect your data

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data you share with us, including restricting access to those who need it to respond to you or deliver your project, and using reputable third-party providers for email and hosting. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information to a standard appropriate to its sensitivity.

12. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business owners and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example as our tools or legal obligations change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version. We encourage you to review it periodically.
